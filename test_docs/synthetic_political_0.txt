A Message to Our Valued Customers

We want to begin by sincerely apologizing to each and every one of our customers who was affected by the service disruption we experienced earlier this week. We know how much you rely on us, and we take that trust extremely seriously.

On Tuesday, some of our customers experienced difficulty accessing certain parts of our service. Our people immediately began working around the clock to address the situation and restore full functionality as quickly as possible. We are happy to report that all services have now been fully restored and are operating normally.

We understand that any interruption to your experience is unacceptable, and we want to assure you that we have already taken steps to make sure this kind of glitch does not happen again. Our teams have conducted a thorough review and have put in place a series of improvements designed to strengthen our resilience and prevent similar disruptions going forward.

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. Your success is our success, and moments like these remind us of the responsibility we carry as your trusted partner. We are deeply committed to providing the reliable, high-quality service you deserve — today, tomorrow, and into the future.

As a gesture of goodwill, affected customers will receive a credit applied automatically to their next billing cycle. You do not need to take any action. If you have questions about your credit or your account, our customer support team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Transparency is one of our core values. We are committed to keeping our customers informed and will continue to share updates through our official communications channels. We are also reviewing our communication processes to ensure that during any future incidents, you receive timely, clear, and helpful information.

We are grateful for your continued patience and loyalty. We do not take your business for granted, and we will work every day to earn back your full confidence. The trust of our customers and our shareholders is something we work hard to protect, and we remain dedicated to delivering the experience you expect from us.

Thank you for being part of our community. We are sorry, and we will do better.

Warm regards,

The Leadership Team
